# Sustained mining at the 2000 MHz clock cap (Balanced default)

The reference numbers the README, the manual, the GUI (`rate.ref`, the setup's preset box) and
`docs/_data/facts.toml` quote for a DGX Spark mining with the default settings. Measured by the owner
on the author's DGX Spark (GB10, 48 SMs, driver 580.178.04, CUDA 13.0) after G1 soak #1 moved the
Balanced clock cap from 2200 to 2000 MHz.

| Setting | Value |
|---|---|
| Work shape | production (m = n = 131072, k = 4096, r = 128, 8×16 hash pattern) |
| Power profile | Balanced (75 W target, 85 W hard stop) |
| SM clock | locked with `nvidia-smi -lgc 300,2000` (the clock-cap unit's value) |

| Result | Value |
|---|---|
| Credited rate, sustained | **73.9 T-MAC/s** (= pool TH/s) |
| GPU power | **~63 W** |
| GPU temperature | **72 °C** |
| Governor duty | 100 % (the cap, not the governor, is what limits power) |
| Credit quantum | 7.04e13 MACs per attempt: the credited rate moves in steps of one attempt |

For comparison, at the 2200 MHz cap (G1 soak #1, 2026-09-26 21:54–22:11 UTC,
`g1-20260926T215417Z-soak.csv` and `g1-20260926T215417Z-summary.txt`): 83–87 W on the GPU, GPU up to
83 °C and a board (`acpitz`) of 97.5 °C, above the governor's 95 °C trip. That is why Balanced uses
2000 MHz and Max (2200 MHz) is opt-in and not recommended. First accepted shares with this miner:
`m6-20260926T222707Z-first-shares.md` (LuckyPool BR, HeroMiners BR, and Kryptex on 2026-09-27).

The register-only tensor-core peak at 2000 MHz is 85.0 T-MAC/s (MB1, `mb1-20260926T185414Z.txt`), so
the sustained 73.9 T-MAC/s is about 87 % of it.

Raw logs of the sustained run are not in the repository; the numbers above are the ones recorded in
the project plan. The next record will be the 60 min and 24 h soaks at 2000 MHz (gate G1,
`bench/g1-soak.sh`).
